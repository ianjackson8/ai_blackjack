# Changelog

One entry per release. The newest entry matches `PLUGIN_META["version"]` in `plugin.py` and the release's git tag (`v` + version, e.g. `v5.1.0`).

- v5.4
  - remote players (Terminal Casino 1.2, GAME_SPEC §3.6): up to 3 Claude Code sessions can sit at your table through the casino's MCP server. `run()` takes `remote`, and `PLUGIN_META["multiplayer"]` declares `seats` with `max_remote` 3
    - they're seated between rounds and play by the same rules and limits: bet or sit out, Perfect Pairs and 21+3 when the table has them, insurance, then hit/stand/double/split. One hand each
    - their view holds only what their seat can see: the dealer's hole card stays `??` until the dealer plays, and the shoe's contents never appear. The README documents the format
    - each player is settled once per round with their net, and a round that never finishes is void
    - on the felt: magenta seats labelled `Name (AI)`, `thinking… 12s` in the seat border and footer, and your `Esc` (leave) and `?` (rules) keys still work while they think. Chat, joins, leaves and timeouts appear in Table Talk, which they receive as events
    - on the clock running out: sit out, no side bets, no insurance, stand
  - seats can be narrower when nobody plays several hands, so four seats fit in one row at 80 columns
  - with `remote` left out (standalone, or a table with no remote seats) everything plays exactly as in 5.3.1
- v5.3.1
  - the deal is quicker with several hands out: the whole deal takes at most 1.2 s, plus 0.3 s to show the side bets (3 hands with side bets took 4.3 s), and any key deals the rest at once
  - a lost side bet is marked under its hand (`PP -$5`, styled like the LOSE badge) instead of looking the same as before the deal
  - the header fits at 80 columns: at least two spaces after the title, and when space runs out the deck count, then the shoe count, then your name are dropped, then the table name is shortened. The table's limits are always shown
- v5.3
  - multi-hand: play up to 3 hands at once against one dealer, each with its own bet (`max_hands` setting / table rule, default 1). Hands sit side by side on the table, and each one doubles, splits and gets its own result; the split limit counts per hand
  - side bets, each switched on separately with its own per-hand min and max:
    - **Perfect Pairs**: your first two cards are a pair. Mixed 6:1, colored 12:1, perfect 25:1
    - **21+3**: your two cards plus the dealer's up card as a poker hand. Flush 5:1, straight 10:1, three of a kind 30:1, straight flush 40:1, suited trips 100:1
    - settled on the deal, shown under each hand, listed in the `?` rules, and totalled in the session summary
  - Terminal Casino: seven new table rules (`max_hands`, `perfect_pairs`, `perfect_pairs_min`, `perfect_pairs_max`, `twenty_one_plus_three`, `twenty_one_plus_three_min`, `twenty_one_plus_three_max`); their defaults leave existing tables unchanged
  - the settings editor gains the new settings, and number settings can now have a maximum
  - a refused double (can't afford it, or after a hit) no longer draws and throws away a card (#34)
  - game logs record each hand's bet and side bets, and which hand each action was on
- v5.2
  - Terminal Casino tables: `run()` takes the casino's `table`; its min/max bet are enforced, its rules (decks, double after split, splits per hand, insurance) override `game_settings.json` for the visit, and the header shows the table name and limits. The visit ends if the player can't cover the table minimum.
  - `PLUGIN_META["rules"]` declares the four table rules
- v5.1
  - dealer peeks for blackjack with an Ace or ten showing: a dealer natural ends the round before anyone acts, so you only lose your original bet (#30)
  - fix crash loading a save after bots were added in settings (#29)
  - fix bets placed this round being lost when saving or `/exit`-ing during betting (#31)
  - player names can no longer match a bot's name (#32)
  - Terminal Casino support: `plugin.py` adapter and a plugin mode (single seat, leave-table key, no money-changing commands)
  - all files (settings, models, saves, logs) are found relative to the game folder, so it can be launched from anywhere
  - at the casino, `?` shows the table rules as well as the commands
  - modules renamed `bj_tui`, `bj_strategy`, `bj_counter`; `sys.exit` only from `__main__`; changelog moved to `CHANGELOG.md`
  - the splash screen shows the real version (read from `PLUGIN_META`)
- v5.0
  - full-screen Rich TUI: card art, seat panels, key-driven prompts (`H`/`S`/`D`/`P`), session summary on exit
  - hit animation: cards slide in face down and flip; totals and log lines wait for the flip (`animate_cards` setting)
  - chip piles that grow with the bet, with animations for betting, doubling, payouts and losing bets
  - side panel with a session scoreboard (sparklines, streaks, biggest win) and "Bot Brains" showing each bot's reasoning; `Tab` toggles the full log
  - in-game settings editor (`Tab` / `/settings` on the name screen)
  - rework the `ai-nn` bot: correct doubling rewards, credit across multi-step hands, soft-hand and can-double features, and a bet-sizing network driven by the true count; retrained from scratch
  - add `counter` bot: basic strategy plus an LSTM that learns to count cards from simulated shoes (`--train-counter`) and ramps bets with the estimated edge
  - add `trial.py`: bot survival trial with balance graph and stats table
  - redraw the screen only when something changes (was a full repaint 8×/s); faster startup
  - fix the shoe running out mid-round, bots looping on an unaffordable double, and `insurance_bet` not being initialised
  - fix payouts: 21 after a split pays 1:1 (only a two-card 21 on an unsplit hand is a blackjack), blackjack vs dealer blackjack pushes, and a dealer blackjack beats a multi-card 21
